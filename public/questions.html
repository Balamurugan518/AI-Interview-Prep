Common Interview Questions

Practice these common interview questions and prepare yourself for your next interview.

01

Tell me about yourself.

Give a short introduction about yourself, your education, skills, experience, and career goals.

General
02

Why should we hire you?

Explain your strongest skills and how you can contribute to the company.

HR
03

What are your strengths?

Talk about your important strengths and provide examples that demonstrate them.

HR
04

What is your biggest weakness?

Mention a genuine area for improvement and explain what you are doing to improve it.

HR
05

Why do you want to join our company?

Explain why the company, role, culture, or opportunity interests you.

HR
06

Where do you see yourself in five years?

Describe your career goals and how the position can help you grow professionally.

Career
07

Explain a difficult project you worked on.

Describe the problem, your approach, the solution, and the result.

Technical
08

How do you handle pressure?

Explain how you organize your work, prioritize tasks, and remain calm.

Behavioral
09

Describe a time you solved a problem.

Explain the situation, the problem, the action you took, and the result.

Problem Solving
10

Do you have any questions for us?

Prepare thoughtful questions about the role, team, company, and opportunities.
